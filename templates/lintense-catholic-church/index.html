about

Our Church

At St. Augustine Church, we want the love that Christ showed on the cross to flow out of our lives, impacting the community. We come from all different backgrounds and cultures but are united in the pursuit and worship of Christ. Above all else, we want to glorify God. We strongly believe that God does His work through the local church and that it is a vital part of the Christian life. Feel free to join us as we come together and let the Gospel transform us!

Meet our Pastor

Thomas Smith

As a long-time serving pastor at St. Augustine, I am committed to serving and helping people that come to our church. Fueled by prayer and the Bible, I am passionate about building the local church and advancing God’s kingdom on Earth. Whatever brings you to St. Augustine, you’ll surely find the support you need to tackle any kind of life struggles and everyday challenges.
