About our Event

Namaste!

Our International Festival has been conceived and held since 2007 as an interesting and lively meeting prepared by yogis and for yogis. In 2021, the 10th anniversary Lintense Awake Yoga Festival. The authors of the idea for the festival are yoga teachers Cameron Williamson and Bessie Cooper. Classes with us are conducted only by high-level professionals, and attending master classes will be most useful and fruitful for you.

Benefits Of Yoga & Mindfullness
Improves flexibility

Yoga poses work by stretching your muscles. They can help you move better.

Increases Strength

Yoga is also a great addition to an exercise routine for its strength-building benefits.

Stress Relief

It is known that yoga significantly reduces stress and promotes relaxation.

Good for Your Heart

Yoga has long been known to lower blood pressure and slow the heart rate.
